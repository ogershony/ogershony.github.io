humanoid

URKL — Humanoid Motion Retargeting

Taught the T800 humanoid to box. Retargeted 50+ unique boxing trajectories — generated by NVIDIA's Kimodo diffusion model — onto the T800 by using Amazon's Holosoma to define a joint mapping between the SOMA and T800 morphologies. Then trained a simulation-realistic motion-tracking policy in MjLab with PPO.

Holosoma Motion Retargeting Diffusion Models MjLab PPO
quadruped

Triton Pupper Locomotion

Reinforcement-learning locomotion for the Triton Pupper quadruped in MuJoCo/MJX. Iterated on reward shaping to progressively refine the gait — the toggle above steps through training iterations 12 → 16 → 20, showing the reward-tuning progression from unstable early attempts to a stable walk.

JAX/RL PPO MuJoCo/MJX Reward Shaping
manipulation

Pick & Place Manipulation

A pick-and-place manipulation task tackled with imitation learning: used mplib to generate motion-planning demonstrations, then trained an MLP to imitate them via behavioral cloning. Also explored solving the task from scratch with pure reinforcement learning.
